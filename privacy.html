Privacy

Niall's Toolkit · a private tool used by NBOYD GROUP LTD

Who uses it

Niall's Toolkit is a personal business tool. It isn't offered to the public, and only its owner signs in to it.

What it stores, and where

Everything you put into the toolkit (sales, bank lines, suppliers, settings and receipts) is stored on your own devices, in the browser or app you use. Nothing is sent to the people who made the toolkit, and there's no server of its own.

Google sign-in

If you connect Google, the toolkit uses your Google account only to:

  • keep a copy of your data in a folder it creates in your own Google Drive ("Niall's Toolkit Sync"), so your devices stay in step;
  • write your figures to a spreadsheet it creates in your Google Drive ("FBA Accounting");
  • file invoices you add in a folder it creates in your Google Drive.

It can only see files and folders it created itself. It doesn't read your email, contacts or any other files, and it doesn't share or sell anything. You can disconnect at any time from the toolkit (Devices & sync → Sign out of Google) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Other services

If you add your own Keepa key, the toolkit asks Keepa for product data directly. Nothing else is contacted.
